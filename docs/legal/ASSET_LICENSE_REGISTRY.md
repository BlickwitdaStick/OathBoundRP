# Asset License Registry

This document is mandatory to keep legal compliance and transparency about every visual and audio resource (meshes, textures, animations, sounds) incorporated into the server's own plugins (`HeavyRP_Equipment.esm`, `HeavyRP_Props.esm`, `HeavyRP_Animations.esm`).

> **Rule:** It is strictly forbidden to extract or redistribute assets from Nexus mods or other sources without the express permission of the original author or a compatible license (e.g. Creative Commons, Open Permissions).

## Procedure for Adding New Assets
1. **License Check:** Check the "Permissions and credits" tab on the mod's original page.
2. **Request:** If permission is closed ("not allowed to upload"), the team must contact the author asking for express permission for packaged distribution in the SkyMP Launcher.
3. **Registration:** After obtaining permission (or confirming an open license), the asset must be registered in the table above before the commit to the main branch.
4. **In-Game Credits:** The official Launcher must contain a "Credits" button that points to this document or presents its data, ensuring continuous recognition of community creators.
